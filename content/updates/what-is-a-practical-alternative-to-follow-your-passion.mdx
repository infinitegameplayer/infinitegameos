Follow a small pull, today. Choose the one thing drawing you a little right now, shrink it until it fits inside an hour and make one small real piece of it before the hour ends. Then write two lines: what you made and what shifted. Lane Belone built this practice into [One Alive Thing](https://www.sidequesthq.co/one-alive-thing), a free one-hour guide at Side Quest HQ.

## Why "follow your passion" stalls

"Follow your passion" puts the passion first. Find the one thing, then follow it. The trouble sits in the first step. Naming a lifelong passion on demand is a question with no material to work from yet. The search becomes the activity while the making waits for an answer.

Cal Newport made the career version of this case in *So Good They Can't Ignore You* (2012): passion tends to follow skill more often than it comes before it. The practical alternative starts from the same place. Make something real first, at a size that fits today.

## A pull is smaller than a passion

A pull is the small draw toward one thing, present now. An idea that keeps coming back. A conversation you keep wanting to have. A sketch, a recipe, a route on a map. It asks for no commitment beyond the next hour. It is available today.

Infinite Game OS names this draw Aliveness: the emerging energy that moves through you when an idea or a moment feels exciting and true to you. It is always present. The variable is what the person follows. A passion is a large and settled version of the same thing. A pull is the version that fits inside one afternoon.

## The practice, in one hour

**The scan.** A short list of what has tugged at you lately, unranked. The one pulling hardest right now is the one to follow. The rest keep for later.

**The size check.** The pull gets shrunk until one hour holds it. Too big: launch the newsletter. Right-sized: record a rough ten-minute voice note on the idea you keep circling.

**The making.** Before the hour ends, one small real thing exists that did not exist this morning.

**The two lines.** What you made. What shifted.

## What the two lines are for

The two lines are a record. Over weeks, the record becomes a map. One made thing is small. A run of them shows which pulls keep returning and which fade after a single hour. A passion, if one is there, shows up in that record as the pull that keeps coming back. It arrives as a pattern in what got made, found after the fact.

## How this fits the Infinite Game

Each hour is a finite game: bounded, small and finished the same day. [Finite games sit inside the Infinite Game](/updates/why-finite-games-sit-inside-the-infinite-game). Here the larger game is the practice itself: following what is alive and making something real from it. That practice keeps running after every hour closes.

"Follow your passion" frames the search as one game to win, with a single answer at the end. The small pull frames it as [the Infinite Game](/infinite-game): a practice that continues, where each finished hour hands something to the next one.
